Privacy & Terms

Vertex keeps your tasks, habits and focus sessions on your own device. This page explains exactly what we hold, what we don't, and the rules for using the app.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version

  • Your tasks and habits never leave your phone. They live in a local database we cannot read.
  • We hold your email and an account ID. That is what an account needs to exist.
  • No ads, no ad identifier, no tracking. The app ships no advertising or analytics SDK.
  • We never sell your data. There is nothing to sell, and we would not.
Privacy Policy Terms of Service

1Who we are

Vertex is a personal productivity app for tasks, habits and focus sessions, developed by Noctascent. Where this page says "we" or "us", it means Noctascent. You can reach us at noctascent@gmail.com.

2What we collect

Only what an account and a working app require:

Information When
Email address When you create an account
Account identifier (a random ID) Generated when your account is created
Display name and profile photo Only if you sign in with Google — provided by Google, read-only
Crash report: error details, device model, OS version, an anonymous install ID Only if the app crashes, and only in public releases

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.

3What stays on your device

Your tasks, habits, habit check-ins, focus sessions, profile details and app settings are stored in a database on your device only. We do not upload, copy or read them, and there is no cloud backup.

Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling Vertex deletes this data permanently, and it does not transfer to a new phone. Use Export Data in Settings before you uninstall or switch devices.

4Exporting your data

Export Data creates a file containing your local data and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes. We never receive a copy.

5Why we hold it

  • Email and account ID — to create your account, sign you in, and keep you signed in.
  • Display name and photo — to show them on your profile screen.
  • Crash reports — to find and fix faults in the app.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone beyond the services in section 6.

6Services we rely on

Two services, both operated by Google:

  • Firebase Authentication — stores your email, account ID and, for Google sign-in, your name and photo. Passwords are stored by Firebase in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — receives crash reports from public releases.

Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also processes the sign-in itself. Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

7Permissions the app requests

Internet access, network state, and access to Google services. All three exist to make sign-in and crash reporting work. The app requests no permission for your camera, location, contacts, storage or advertising identifier.

8How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Deleting your account removes it from Firebase Authentication straight away, and wipes your tasks, habits, check-ins, focus sessions and profile from the device you delete it on.

9Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or send it to you in a portable form. Email noctascent@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

To delete your account yourself, open Settings (the gear on your Profile) and choose Delete account. You will be asked to confirm, and to sign in again to prove it is you. Deletion is free, immediate and permanent. Your theme and focus-timer preferences stay on the device, because they belong to the device rather than to your account. If you can no longer sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are performance of a contract for account data, and legitimate interest for crash reports. You may also object to processing, request restriction, or complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in Saudi Arabia, this policy is written to align with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

10Age

Vertex is intended for people aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it. In countries where the age of digital consent is higher than 13, you need a parent or guardian's consent below that age.

11Security

Account data travels over encrypted connections and is stored by Firebase encrypted at rest. Your device data is protected by your device's own security. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and your device locked.

12Changes to this policy

We will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects what we collect or why, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

13Contact
